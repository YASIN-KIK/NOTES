# Privacy Policy — Notes App

Last updated: 2026

## Data collection
The Notes app stores all notes **locally on your device**. Notes are not transmitted to any server unless you explicitly use the optional sign-up.

## Optional sign-up
On first launch, you may optionally provide your name and email. This is **not required** — you can skip it and use the app fully offline. If you sign up, the name and email are sent to the developer's backend and stored solely to understand usage. No note content is ever transmitted.

## AI Assistant
If you use the AI Assistant, your prompts and note content are sent to the AI provider you configure (e.g., OpenAI) using your own API key. The developer does not receive this data. See your provider's privacy policy.

## Third parties
- AI providers (only when you use the AI Assistant, with your own key)
- Microsoft Store (for app distribution and updates)
